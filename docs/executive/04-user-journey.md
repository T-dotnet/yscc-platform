## From first contact to a clear next step.

A care episode holds the whole period of engagement. An individual response is only one contribution within it.

1. **Intake and triage.** Every new patient completes intake; record triage outcome and owner before assessment.
2. **Explain participation.** Check purpose, authority, visibility and suitable contact.
3. **Plan the assessment.** Use the proposed shared core and clinically eligible modules.
4. **Choose respondent and mode.** Confirm who answers, who records, assistance and channel eligibility.
5. **Complete and submit.** Save truthfully; confirm the accepted response for this assignment.
6. **Review the evidence.** Keep missing work visible; add further eligible modules if needed.
7. **Decide and hand over.** Track referral sending, receipt, acceptance/decline and handover; keep follow-up owned.
8. **Continue care and review.** Create distinct repeat collections inside the same episode.
9. **Close or transition.** Record next care action and reconcile pending requests and reminders.

### The journey includes loops and exits

More evidence returns to steps 2-5. A pause or incomplete exit records reason, owner and next action. Only continuing care enters recurring collection; one refusal does not automatically close care.

### What leadership must validate

Mandatory new-patient intake is confirmed (D-25). Finalise intake fields/triage and external handover rules (D-26/D-27). Repeated reviews stay in the same episode.

Basis: Detailed brief 04, J-01 to J-09; U1/D-25 confirms intake. Clinical/service detail remains D-26/D-27.

## Close the learning loop.

The CMDCS ambition extends beyond collection: evidence should produce accountable action and return to services.

### Service Improvement

RACHEL / TOM / ANANYA / SAM

1. **Establish.** Agree readiness, definitions, roles and configuration.
2. **Capture and check.** Resolve quality issues with evidence and clear ownership.
3. **Understand.** Interpret performance using cohort, quality and local context.
4. **Improve.** Agree an action and follow-up; assess what changed.

### System Evidence

PRIYA / DAVID / MAYA / HELEN

1. **Define the request.** Specify purpose, product, recipient and authority.
2. **Review and prepare.** Validate data, permissions, disclosure and release conditions.
3. **Receive and interpret.** Use only the approved report, exchange or study release.
4. **Return findings.** Assign service or governance action; close use under policy.

> A report is not the end of the journey. The next action, owner and feedback to care must be visible.

### Across the lifecycle

Support, permission changes, corrections and handover apply whenever needed. Services and satisfaction are conditional. Wider care plans and personal progress remain candidate capabilities.

### Approve the handoffs

For each wider data product, confirm who requests it, who approves release, who acts on the finding and how learning returns. Do not grant care-record access simply to close a reporting loop.

Basis: Detailed brief 04, SJ-01 to SJ-08; CP1; CR-01 to CR-08. Wider workflows require D-22 to D-24.
