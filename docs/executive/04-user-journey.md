## From first contact to a clear next step.

A care episode holds the whole period of engagement. An individual response is only one contribution within it.

- 01 - Intake and triage - Every new patient completes intake; record triage outcome and owner before assessment.

- 02 - Explain participation - Check purpose, authority, visibility and suitable contact.

- 03 - Plan the assessment - Use the proposed shared core and clinically eligible modules.

- 04 - Choose respondent and mode - Confirm who answers, who records, assistance and channel eligibility.

- 05 - Complete and submit - Save truthfully; confirm the accepted response for this assignment.

- 06 - Review the evidence - Keep missing work visible; add further eligible modules if needed.

- 07 - Decide and hand over - Track referral sending, receipt, acceptance/decline and handover; keep follow-up owned.

- 08 - Continue care and review - Create distinct repeat collections inside the same episode.

- 09 - Close or transition - Record next care action and reconcile pending requests and reminders.

### The journey includes loops and exits

The local prototype demonstrates these states with fictional data, including purpose-specific consent and policy-dependent review. More evidence returns to steps 2-5; a pause or incomplete exit retains owner and next action.

### WHAT LEADERSHIP MUST VALIDATE

The local prototype demonstrates intake, referral follow-through, consent requests, review-required/not-required states and repeat collection in one episode. Finalise D-04-D-10 and D-26/D-27.

## Close the learning loop.

The CMDCS ambition extends beyond collection: evidence should produce accountable action and return to services.

### Service Improvement

**Tag:** RACHEL / TOM / ANANYA / SAM

- **Establish:** Agree readiness, definitions, roles and configuration.

- **Capture and check:** Resolve quality issues with evidence and clear ownership.

- **Understand:** Interpret performance using cohort, quality and local context.

- **Improve:** Agree an action and follow-up; assess what changed.

### System Evidence

**Tag:** PRIYA / DAVID / MAYA / HELEN

- **Define the request:** Specify purpose, product, recipient and authority.

- **Review and prepare:** Validate data, permissions, disclosure and release conditions.

- **Receive and interpret:** Use only the approved report, exchange or study release.

- **Return findings:** Assign service or governance action; close use under policy.

A report is not the end of the journey. The next action, owner and feedback to care must be visible.

### Across the lifecycle

Support, purpose-specific consent requests and withdrawals, corrections and handover apply whenever needed. Services and satisfaction are conditional. Wider care plans and participant progress remain candidate capabilities.

### APPROVE THE HANDOFFS

For each wider data product, confirm who requests it, who approves release, who acts on the finding and how learning returns. Do not grant care-record access simply to close a reporting loop.

Basis: Detailed brief 04, SJ-01 to SJ-08; CP1; CR-01 to CR-08. Wider workflows require D-22 to D-24.
